# IoT Explorer Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-20_

IoT Explorer is a desktop application for inspecting IoT protocol traffic.
It works entirely on your computer.

## What we collect

Nothing. IoT Explorer has no account system, no analytics and no telemetry.
It does not send any data to the developer or to third parties.

## What stays on your computer

Connections, targets, alerts, schedules, captured messages and preferences
are stored only in the application's local database on your computer. They
never leave it unless you export them yourself.

## Network access

The application connects only to the brokers, devices and servers you
configure, using the protocols you choose (MQTT, CoAP, OPC UA, Modbus and
the others listed in the app). Listening connections accept traffic only on
the address and port you set.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the Mac App Store.
The application asks StoreKit which subscriptions are active and stores that
answer locally so it can start offline. Payment details are handled by Apple
and are never visible to the application. Apple's privacy policy applies to
that processing: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
